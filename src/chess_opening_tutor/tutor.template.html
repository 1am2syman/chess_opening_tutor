How to study

Before revealing a move, ask what the position needs. Read the takeaway, then compare a candidate line. In recall mode, play White’s moves by tapping a piece and destination. A legal alternative is not automatically a mistake.

Arrow keys navigate moves. Home/End jump to the start/end. Details may expand the page; with them collapsed, board and controls fit the iPhone 11 layout.
